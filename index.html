Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your first training evaluation.

At what age should puppy training officially start?
You can begin our Puppy Foundation curriculum as early as 8 weeks old. Early socialization and bite inhibition between weeks 8 and 16 represent the most critical developmental window of a dog's life.
Do you use shock collars or harsh punitive methods?
No. We strictly adhere to positive reinforcement and LIMA (Least Intrusive, Minimally Aversive) guidelines. We motivate dogs through food rewards, play drive, and clear environmental markers. We never cause pain, fear, or mental distress.
Is my dog too old or too stubborn to learn?
The old saying that 'you can't teach an old dog new tricks' is false! Older dogs often have better focus and attention spans than young puppies. As long as a dog is medically sound, they can enthusiastically learn new manners at 5, 8, or 10 years old.
Can you fix intense reactivity or leash barking?
Yes, our Behavioral Bootcamp is designed specifically for reactive dogs. We use systematic desensitization and counter-conditioning to change the dog's underlying emotional reaction to triggers rather than just masking the symptoms.
What happens if my dog relapses after training?
Every graduate receives access to our monthly alumni practice pack sessions and lifetime email support. If you ever hit a rough patch, our trainers provide immediate troubleshooting advice to get you back on track.
